The goal of this lab is to become an expert inside the network configuration field and troubleshooting skills.
It allows you to simulate different networks without having to use the physical equipment.
In addition you can learn and then practise your IOT and cybersecurity skills.
You can choose to build networks from scratch. The course focuses on exploring packet tracer
interface, including menus, icons and devices that are used in networking. The main idea of this
lab is to get familiar with a small business network.
